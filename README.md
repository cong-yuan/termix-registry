## How to Add in Termix

1. Open Termix, go to **Plugins** ➔ **Registries**.
2. Click **Add registry**.
3. Enter the URL:
   ```text
   https://cong-yuan.github.io/termix-registry/index.json
   ```
4. Click **Check** to verify the fingerprint (`c9bf7f4c905420b5`).
5. Click **Trust and add**.
